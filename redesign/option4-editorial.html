Who am I

I wrote my first code at seven years old and have never stopped building, iterating, hacking and pushing the boundaries of what's possible. I'm a technologist, developer and CTO with a lifelong passion for coding, problem-solving and team building.

💡 Driven by an insatiable curiosity and an appetite for solving complex real-world problems. 🌟 My interests revolve around building systems where the complex interactions of different participants result in a positive-sum outcome for all.

🚀 I iterate rapidly, fail fast, and imbue everything with instrumentation and metrics. A tight cycle of building, measuring and learning — then repeating with learnings — is the strongest indicator of an effective team I know.
