About

💡 Driven by insatiable curiosity and an appetite for solving complex real-world problems.

🌟 My interests revolve around building systems where the complex interactions of different participants result in a positive-sum outcome for all.

🚀 I believe in iterating rapidly, failing fast and imbuing everything with instrumentation and metrics. The tight cycle of building, measuring, learning — then repeating with learnings — is the strongest indicator of an effective team.

"The real test of any skill is condensing complex technical elements into clearly articulable forms."

🧐 A firm advocate of the KISS principle — whether in architecture, system design or software development.

Career

Served as CTO for several years at Rhinofi (formerly DeversiFi), navigating several shifts within web3 across previous market cycles.

There I built the high-performance matching engine and execution infrastructure that processed >$1.5 billion USD in volume without issue and powered the first production-ready Layer 2 rollup DEX back in 2020. Modular liquidity sources underpinned the markets — one being the first AMM to blend price curves with dynamically quantised orderbook liquidity — giving users a CEX-grade trading experience with decentralisation, self-custody and trustlessness.

Whenever possible I attend tech and web3 events around the world to collaborate, share knowledge on decentralisation, self-sovereignty and trustlessness, and drive discussions around the future of solver-based execution services.
